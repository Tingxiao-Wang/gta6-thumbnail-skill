## Suggested first benchmark / 首轮验收

Use the same host model, uploaded assets, exact headline and platform with and without the Skill, then visually compare:

- Viewer hook and visual story, rather than extra copy.
- Non-textual design and subject/scene relationships.
- Distinct A/B/C scene architectures.
- Accurate, legible, visually impactful title hierarchy.
- Privacy-safe preservation of a supplied real-person photo.

No claim of proven CTR improvement is made.
